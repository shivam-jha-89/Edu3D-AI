      HoloLearn AI

      TOUCH KNOWLEDGE · EXPLORE REALITY

      An AI-powered interactive 3D learning laboratory where you explore anatomy, biology, engineering, and world landmarks through glowing GPU particle holograms controlled directly by your bare hands.

      SEE → TOUCH → EXPLORE → UNDERSTAND
      1. Snap 🫰 your fingers or press Space to activate the holographic particle field.
      2. Make a fist ✊ to materialize the 3D educational model.
      3. Open ✋ your hand: organs and machines explode outward to reveal every internal component with its function and biological/engineering role.
      4. Twist 🔄 your hand to rotate the model; raise or lower to tilt; use two hands 🙌 to zoom.
      5. Point ☝ at any component to inspect it, and pinch 🤏 to pull it out.
      6. Engage Holo X-Ray ✂ to inspect cross-sections, Holo Tutor 🤖 for explanations, and Holo Quiz 🎓 to test your knowledge!
      🔒 Camera processing occurs locally in your browser. Video is never uploaded.
